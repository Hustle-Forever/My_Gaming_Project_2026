Documentation

Up and running in 5 minutes

STEP 01

Create your account

Sign up free — no card needed. You get your own dashboard instantly.

STEP 02

Add your AI key

Paste your provider key in the dashboard. It's encrypted and only ever used for your server.

STEP 03

Install the resource

Drop the M2 folder into your server, add two lines to server.cfg, restart. No ports to open.

STEP 04

Start commanding

Open M2 on your phone, hit the mic, and speak. Your city responds.

Does this work with QBCore and ESX?
Yes. M2 runs as a standard FiveM resource and works alongside QBCore, ESX, or a standalone setup.
Do I need to open ports on my server?
No. The M2 resource only makes outbound requests, so there's nothing to configure on your firewall or host panel.
Can M2 do anything I don't want it to?
No. M2 can only perform actions from a fixed, approved list defined for your server. Anything outside that list is rejected before it ever reaches your game.
Which languages are supported?
Arabic and English, both by voice and text. The whole interface flips to right-to-left in Arabic.
Is my AI key safe?
Your key is encrypted at rest and is never sent to the browser or shown again after you save it.
